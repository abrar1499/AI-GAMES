BOLTA MUNSHI: ElevenLabs ke liye alfaaz
=========================================

Kul alfaaz: 265, 9 hisson mein.

Kaise banayein:
1. ElevenLabs website par Text to Speech kholein.
2. Model: "Eleven Multilingual v2" chunein. Awaaz: jo aap chahte hain.
3. Neeche har hissa ("HISSA 1" waghera) ka poora text copy kar ke paste karein,
   aur Generate karein. <break time="1.0s" /> ko mat hatayein: is se har lafz ke baad
   1 second ki khamoshi aati hai, jis se main lafz alag alag kaat sakunga.
4. Har hisse ki audio download karein aur naam hisse ke number par rakhein:
   01.mp3, 02.mp3 ... 09.mp3 (mp3 ya wav, dono chalenge).
5. Saari files mujhe bhej dein. Main khamoshi kaat kar, awaaz barabar kar ke,
   har lafz ko app mein daal dunga.

Dhyan rahe:
- Har hisse mein ek bhi lafz kam ya zyada na ho, aur tarteeb na badlein.
- Agar audio mein "break" ka lafz bolta sunai de, to mujhe batayein.
- Settings (Stability / Similarity) har hisse ke liye ek jaisi rakhein,
  taa ke saare lafz ek jaisi awaaz mein hon.
